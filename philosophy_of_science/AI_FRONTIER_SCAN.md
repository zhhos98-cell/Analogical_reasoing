# AI Frontier Scan — Analogical Transfer

Snapshot: 21 August 2026.

This note tracks direct AI evidence adjacent to the philosophy-of-science branch.

## Current pattern

AI systems are increasingly good at candidate analogue retrieval and structural alignment, but evaluation still shows a gap between finding a plausible relation and licensing a target projection.

## Key evidence clusters

### 1. Retrieval/alignment strength

Recent LLM studies show that models can encode relational structures in analogy tasks, but failures often appear when relations must be transferred to genuinely novel entities rather than simply recognized.

### 2. Precision versus recall

Strategic analogy studies suggest a recurring tradeoff: LLMs can generate many candidate analogies, while contextual matching remains harder. This maps directly onto the philosophy-of-science distinction between mapping and projection relevance.

### 3. Transfer scaffolding

Analogy-guided reasoning methods show gains when models are explicitly directed to retrieve and reflect on structurally related examples. The unresolved question is whether this improves only task accuracy or also calibrated transfer validity.
