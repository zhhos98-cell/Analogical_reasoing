# Active Research Program and AI Bridge

**Snapshot: 21 August 2026.**

The contemporary philosophy-of-science material on analogy is not merely a loose set of papers. There is a visible research network connecting philosophy of physics, general philosophy of science, extrapolation and AI.

## 1. PRIN — Analogical Reasoning in Contemporary Physical Theories

**PI: Giovanni Valente.** Collaboration between Politecnico di Milano and Università degli Studi di Urbino Carlo Bo; funding period December 2023–December 2025.

Project page: https://www.meta.polimi.it/analogical-reasoning-in-contemporary-physical-theories/

The project explicitly asks under what conditions plausible analogical inferences can be drawn in contemporary physics, particularly when target systems are empirically difficult to access, including subatomic physics and black holes.

The project frames analogy as relevant both to:

```text
discovery / hypothesis formation
and
justification / confirmation.
```

## 2. Analogies in Physics and Beyond, Politecnico di Milano, November 2024

Conference page: https://www.meta.polimi.it/conference-analogies-in-physics-and-beyond/

The programme makes the interdisciplinary structure unusually explicit.

Physics side included:

- spontaneous symmetry breaking;
- quantum analogy;
- classical vs quantum computation;
- analogue confirmation of Hawking radiation.

The `and Beyond` day included:

- similarity in interactive biorobotics;
- extrapolation, analogy and comparative process tracing;
- templates/models in synthetic biology;
- **analogical proportions in AI**.

This is strong evidence that the philosophy-of-science community itself is treating computational AI analogy and scientific analogy as neighboring problems.

## 3. Cambridge Element 2026

**Francesco Nappo & Giovanni Valente, _Analogical Reasoning in Science_.**  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1017/9781009526494

Published online 8 June 2026 in Cambridge Elements in the Philosophy of Science.

The Element presents the exact epistemological role of analogy in science as an outstanding contemporary problem and surveys natural/social-science cases.

For this repo it should function as a **field orientation node**, not as a final authority.

## 4. Direct philosophy→AI bridge: Votsis

**Ioannis Votsis, _Modelling Analogical Reasoning: One-Size-Fits-All?_, in _Wittgenstein and Artificial Intelligence_ (2024; Cambridge online listing 2025).**

The chapter starts from an explicit AI motivation:

```text
if scientific reasoning is to be automated,
AI needs models that distinguish good analogical inference from bad.
```

It contrasts:

- universal models of analogy;
- local/context-sensitive models;

and explores whether a universal framework can survive if supplemented by additional relevance/uniformity criteria.

This makes the universal-vs-local issue an explicitly computational one, not merely a philosophical taxonomy.

## 5. A useful emerging division of labor

### Philosophy of science asks

```text
What makes a similarity relevant?
What evidence supports the transfer premise?
What kind of confirmation is obtained?
What makes several sources genuinely robust?
When is a source externally validated?
How should uncertainty/difference constrain extrapolation?
```

### Current AI asks

```text
How are relations represented?
How do we retrieve far sources?
How do we align structures?
How do we train reusable procedures?
How do we diagnose hidden-state mapping failures?
```

The two communities increasingly meet at:

```text
TRANSFER CONTROL.
```

## 6. The repo's bridge hypothesis

A plausible research program is:

```text
modern AI supplies:
  endogenous representation
  large-scale retrieval
  learned mapping
  continual memory

contemporary PoS supplies:
  bridge/relevance conditions
  extrapolation premises
  external validation
  robustness/dependence constraints
  uncertainty / abstention logic
  local evidential norms
```

The empirical question is whether these philosophical distinctions provide **incremental performance/calibration** when turned into training targets or controller states.

## 7. Contact/read radar, not outreach yet

Current clusters worth tracking:

- **Politecnico di Milano / Urbino** — Valente, Nappo and the PRIN analogue-reasoning network;
- **analogue gravity / philosophy of physics** — Crowther, Thébault, Hartmann and adjacent confirmation debates;
- **extrapolation / external validity** — Khosrowi, Fuller, Guala and mechanism/transportability work;
- **formal analogical inference** — Gebharter, Osimani, Zwirn & Zwirn, Douven and collaborators;
- **philosophy–AI bridge** — Votsis and researchers connecting scientific representation, neuro-symbolic discovery and analogical modelling.

The next threshold for outreach should be an empirical object — benchmark result, transfer-head prototype or clear failure set — rather than a literature map alone.

## Bottom line

There is a real contemporary philosophy-of-science program around scientific analogy, and it is already brushing directly against AI.

The repo therefore should not treat philosophy of science as historical prior art. It is a **parallel live research community working on the normative half of the same transfer problem**.
